Sound advice and traps: what the research says

What excellent executives do

  • Decide earlier, and match the process to how reversible the decision is.
  • Thank the first person who brings bad news.
  • Tell the board chair before the meeting, never during it.
  • Act on stated values in a crisis before all the facts are in.
  • Judge every use of capital by long-term value per share.
  • Change incentives together with the message.

Advice to avoid

  • “Never decide until you have all the facts.”
  • “My door is always open, so people will tell me.”
  • “Don’t apologise: it admits liability.”
  • “A board of famous names protects the company.”
  • “Fix misconduct with more ethics training.”
  • “Announce your vision in week one.”

Questions

Is this an accredited qualification?

No. C-Suite Coach Institute is a private executive-education program. Its certificates are issued on the Institute’s own authority and verified on its own register. They show that you completed a demanding course of study and examination; they are not university degrees or regulated professional licences.

Who is it for?

Current C-suite executives, the vice-presidents and directors preparing for the role, founders who suddenly have a board, and anyone who wants to understand how good executives think.

How long does a certificate take?

Executive Foundations is about 2 hours of study plus your drill sessions over a week or two; each role program is about 1 to 1.5 hours. Spaced repetition works best a little every day.

How do the examinations work?

Every module ends with a scenario challenge. The final examination combines a recall round of 12 items drawn from the whole program (80% to pass) with a capstone scenario scored against a rubric (70% to pass). Both are self-assessed under an honour code; you can retake after 30 minutes.

Where does the content come from?

From published research and public reporting: McKinsey’s CEO Excellence study, the CEO Genome Project in Harvard Business Review, Spencer Stuart, Deloitte, NACD, the work of Jim Collins and Amy Edmondson, regulators’ rules, and well-documented cases. Every module lists its sources. No affiliation with or endorsement by these organisations is implied.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Enrol with an email address and start studying.

Who can see my progress?

Only you. If you opt in to the Honour Roll, your name, job title and credentials appear there. Anyone with a credential code can verify it; that is what a credential is for.
